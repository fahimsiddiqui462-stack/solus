NEED HELP?

Frequently
Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about
our services, therapy, and mental well-
being.

How do I book a therapy session?

Online booking is not enabled yet. Visit our Therapists page to explore support options, then use the Contact page for general inquiries.

Are online sessions available?

Online availability depends on the professional and your location. Ask about availability before arranging a session.

What’s the difference between therapy and coaching?

Therapy provides clinical support from an appropriately qualified professional. Coaching focuses on goals and habits and is not a substitute for clinical care.

Do I need a subscription to access services?

Subscription and fee information is not published yet. Contact us to ask about available options and costs.

Can I switch therapists if I don’t feel the right connection?

A good fit matters. Discuss your preferences and options with your provider before deciding whether to change therapists.

Is my information and session history kept confidential?

Ask your provider about confidentiality, record storage, and any legal exceptions. Do not send medical records or sensitive clinical information through our general contact form.
